The Analyst Files · Case #04
THE MISSING ORDERS
Fashion E-commerce

Setup

You're a Product Analyst on a Myntra-style marketplace. An alert lands: confirmed orders fell from 5% to 3% of sessions over three days — a 2-point drop that at this scale is crores of rupees. Leadership wants an answer. No error message, no obvious culprit, traffic looks normal.

Evidence

  • Order conversion dropped 5% → 3% — a relative decline of 40% in three days.
  • No deploys are flagged in the release log for the last 72 hours.
  • App traffic and page-load times look unchanged on the monitoring dashboard.

The Mystery

Before you open a single dashboard or write one query — what is your very first move?

Make Your Move

Choose your first move before continuing. There's no need to know the answer yet — tap an option to see why it's worth considering.

Why this move

Every option is reasonable — but only one is the correct FIRST step in a structured RCA.

Today you'll learn the RCA life cycle and follow it through the whole Myntra case. The right sequence — not the right instinct — separates panic from method.

🎯 Your mission: come to class with a first move in mind.

Real-World Use Cases

RCA Life CycleWhen Zomato's order-acceptance rate drops on a weekend, the on-call analyst runs the same lifecycle — clarify, rule out tracking, check partner issues, then check gateway saturation.
Establishing AssumptionsAmazon's teams define “conversion” three ways (add-to-cart, checkout-start, purchase) before any comparison — an RCA on the wrong definition points the wrong way.
User Journey FunnelFlipkart's Big Billion Days team maps the checkout funnel 48h before launch. Any step above a drop-off threshold triggers investigation — before the sale, not after.
EstimationA 1% checkout drop on ₹100 Cr/day GMV = ₹1 Cr/day at risk. Putting a rupee figure on it decides whether the CEO gets involved or just the PM.
Clarifying QuestionsA ride app's cancellation spikes. Driver- or rider-initiated? City-wide or one corridor? Five questions cut 50 possible causes down to 3.
Payment IssuesGateway failures are the single most common cause of sudden conversion drops in Indian e-commerce — a bank's 2-hour net-banking outage can drop conversion 30%, invisible in traffic data.

CRIED Framework
Today's RCA life cycle is CRIED at full depth on a live case. Clarify → Rule Out → Internal → External is the spine — you'll walk it in real time.
User Journey Funnel
The acquisition → engagement → conversion funnel underpins today's step-level diagnosis. Mentally walk a user from landing to placing an order.
Metric Definition
You spent Lecture 3 defining what each metric tracks. A loosely defined metric (“session” vs. “authenticated session”) can derail an entire RCA.
Vanity vs. Actionable
During a crisis, vanity metrics like total traffic can mask the real problem. Knowing which signals to trust under pressure matters acutely today.
